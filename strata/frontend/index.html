From orbit to ore body

Each target coordinate passes through multi-tier satellite and terrain processing before the trained Random Forest classifier evaluates its mineralization confidence.

01

Satellite data ingestion

Multispectral and hyperspectral passes are pulled from Sentinel-2 (VNIR & SWIR), Landsat 8/9, ASTER, and EnMAP alongside SRTM digital elevation models.

Sentinel-2 MSILandsat 8/9 OLIASTER SWIRSRTM DEM
02

Spectral & geophysical feature extraction

Surface reflectance atmospheric corrections isolate ferric iron oxide staining (B4/B2), gossan caps (B4/B3), laterite crusts (B11/B8), and mask dense canopy via NDVI.

03

Machine Learning reserve prediction

Scikit-Learn Random Forest ensemble model evaluated with 300 estimators, balanced class weighting, and strict validation cross-checks against Geological Survey of India deposits.

04

Geological proximity calibration

Point coordinates are referenced against verified USGS MRDS and GSI Bhukosh records across Sausar, Iron Ore, and Dharwar Supergroup manganese formations.
